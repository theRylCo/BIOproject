# BIOproject

1. Top section

At the top, you can:

read the main theme
use the nav bar to jump to sections
click:
Launch ecosystem simulator
Try conservation challenge

The nav links scroll you to the matching section.

2. Problem section

This section is about human pressure over time.

Use the year slider to move from the past into the future.
As you drag it, the site updates:

the year
estimated global population
habitat indicator
pressure level

This gives a quick visual of how pressure changes over time.

3. History section

This section is the conservation timeline.

On the left, click different timeline cards like:

Age of exploitation
Dawn of conservation
Environmental movement
Modern conservation biology

When you click one, the right side updates with:

a visual icon
a title
a short explanation
key ideas
4. Biodiversity section

This section has tabs.

Click between:

Species diversity
Genetic diversity
Ecosystem diversity
Functional diversity

Each tab changes:

the visual box on the left
the title and explanation on the right
the key term cards

Use this section to compare different levels of biodiversity.

5. Hotspots section

This is the map section.

Click the glowing markers on the map.
Each marker opens info for a hotspot like:

Amazon
California
Madagascar
Sundaland
Reef
Andes

When you click one, the panel updates with:

hotspot title
description
summary stats
threat bars
6. Ecosystem simulator

This is one of the main interactive parts.

First choose a biome:

Forest
Wetland
Grassland
Reef

Then adjust the sliders:

habitat protection
climate stress
pollution
hunting / harvest pressure
connectivity / corridors
invasive pressure

As you change them, the site updates:

biodiversity health
ecosystem services
resilience
extinction pressure
diagnosis text
landscape visuals

Higher protection/connectivity usually help.
Higher stress/pollution/invasives usually hurt.

7. Ecological footprint section

This part uses dropdown menus.

Change:

Diet
Transport
Home energy
Consumption

The site updates:

the “Earths if everyone lived this way” number
the circular chart
the category breakdown
the interpretation text

Use Reset to baseline to return to default values.

8. Ethics section

This section lets you compare ethical frameworks.

First choose a framework on the left:

Consequential / utilitarian
Preservation ethic
Land ethic

Then choose a decision in the scenario:

Rebuild at full scale
Partial restoration
Remove the dam

The right side updates with:

framework reading
decision result

This shows how different value systems can lead to different conservation choices.

9. Extinction risk section

This section simulates species risk.

First choose a species type on the left.
Then adjust:

population trend
range size
habitat connectivity

The site updates:

conservation status
summary text
progress bars

Possible outcomes include:

Least Concern
Near Threatened
Threatened
Endangered
Critically Endangered
10. Final challenge section

This is the budget allocation part.

You have budget sliders for:

habitat protection
restoration
research and monitoring
anti-poaching / law enforcement
community incentives
captive breeding

As you move them, the site calculates:

species recovery
habitat integrity
social durability
long-term resilience

It also gives you a final strategy label and explanation.

Try to stay at or below 100 / 100 for a realistic plan.

11. Quiz section

This is the review section.

Click one answer for each question.
The site will:

mark correct answers
mark wrong selected answers
update the score ring

Click Reset quiz to start over.

12. Back to top

At the bottom, click Back to top to return to the beginning of the page.

Best way to demo the site

A smooth way to present it is:

Start with the Problem year slider
Show the History timeline
Compare tabs in Biodiversity
Click a few Hotspots
Spend most time in the Simulator
Show the Ethics tradeoff section
Finish with the Budget challenge and Quiz
